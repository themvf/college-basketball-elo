## Understanding the Metrics

**Accuracy**: Percentage of games where we correctly predicted the winner.

**Brier Score**: Measures the accuracy of probabilistic predictions. Lower is better. Perfect predictions = 0.0, random guessing ≈ 0.25.

**Log Loss**: Measures how well our predicted probabilities match actual outcomes. Lower is better. Perfect predictions = 0.0.

**Spread Error**: Average difference between our predicted point spread and the actual margin of victory.

**Beat Vegas**: Percentage of games where our spread prediction was more accurate than the Vegas spread.

**Confidence Stratified**: When we predict a team has a 70-80% chance to win, they should actually win about 70-80% of the time. This table shows our calibration.
